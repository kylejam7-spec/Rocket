# Third-party assets

Most of these are CC0 (public domain) and need no credit; the table below lists everything.
The ones whose licence asks for credit are listed under Credits.

## Credits

Required by the licence (CC BY 4.0, which also asks that changes be noted). Put these
lines in the experience description:

    Sound credits: "Spaceship Fly-by, A" by InspectorJ (freesound.org/s/397948),
    CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0), trimmed and mixed to mono.

That is the Saturn UFO's fly-by (cue `saturn/flyby`, built by `tools/sounds/build.py`).

Not required, but appreciated: the Saturn UFO's impact by AudioPapkin and its beeps by
SkySpeira (both CC0, freesound.org).
